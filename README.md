## 💖 About

**TruShine** is a cozy little world that lives right on your desktop.

Two best friends accompany you throughout the day, bringing personality, warmth, and delightful interactions to your screen. Whether they're walking around, waving hello, sharing flowers, taking naps, or reacting to your cursor, they make every moment feel a little more alive.

---

## 🌟 Features

✨ Always-on-top transparent desktop companion

🖱️ Cursor interaction and reaction system

🚶 Smooth walking and movement animations

😴 Sleep, idle, sit, and peek behaviors

💬 Cute speech bubbles and messages

❤️ Wholesome dual-character interactions

🎉 Random playful animations

⚙️ Customizable settings panel

🔊 Optional sound effects

💾 Persistent user preferences

🖥️ Windows 10 & Windows 11 support

---

## 💕 Character Moments

The companions can:

- Walk together
- Sit together
- Sleep together
- Wave at you
- Follow your cursor
- Share flowers
- Celebrate achievements
- Peek from screen edges
- React to clicks
- Brighten your workspace
